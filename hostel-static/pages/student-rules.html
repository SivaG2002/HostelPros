Hostel Rules & Regulations

📋 General Rules

  • 1. Curfew Timing: All students must be in their rooms by 10:00 PM (weekdays) and 11:00 PM (weekends).
  • 2. Guest Policy: Guests allowed 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Weekend parental visits require prior intimation.
  • 3. Room Cleanliness: Rooms must be kept clean. Weekly inspections will be conducted.
  • 4. Electronic Appliances: Only registered appliances allowed. Heavy load devices are prohibited.

🤝 Code of Conduct

  • 1. Respect & Discipline: Maintain respectful behavior towards staff and residents.
  • 2. No Loud Noise: Quiet hours from 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM.
  • 3. Substance Prohibition: Smoking, alcohol, and drugs strictly prohibited.
  • 4. Item Safety: Hostel not responsible for personal belongings.

🚨 Safety & Emergency

  • 1. Emergency Evacuation: Know exits and participate in drills.
  • 2. Fire Safety: Do not tamper with safety equipment.
  • 3. Medical Emergency: Report emergencies to warden immediately.

🍽️ Mess Management

  • 1. Meal Timings: Breakfast 7:00–8:30 | Lunch 1:00–2:00 | Dinner 7:30–9:00
  • 2. Leave Intimation: Inform warden 2 days prior for mess leave.
  • 3. Dietary Restrictions: Special dietary requests available upon registration.

💰 Fees & Payment

  • 1. Payment Due: Fees due by 10th of every month.
  • 2. Late Payment: Late fees apply after due date.
  • 3. Damage Charges: Property damage charged to student account.

📞 Grievance Redressal

If you have complaints:

  • Contact block warden
  • File complaint online
  • Visit hostel administration office
