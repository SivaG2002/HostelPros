Our Hostel Facilities

We provide a comprehensive range of facilities to ensure a safe, comfortable, and enriching hostel experience. Every amenity is thoughtfully designed to support your academic journey, well-being, and personal growth.

Accommodation & Rooms
Spacious, well-ventilated rooms with comfortable beds, study desks, ample storage, and natural light. Single, double, and triple sharing options available, all maintained to high standards for privacy and comfort.
Security & Surveillance
24/7 security with trained staff, CCTV surveillance in common areas, secure entry/exit protocols, and visitor management to ensure a safe and protected environment for all residents.
Food & Dining
Nutritious, hygienic meals served in a spacious dining hall. Weekly menu with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, special diet accommodations, and regular quality checks for taste and safety.
Internet & Study Areas
High-speed WiFi throughout the hostel, dedicated study rooms, and quiet zones for focused learning, group discussions, and online research, supporting academic excellence.
Utilities & Maintenance
Reliable power backup, purified drinking water, regular cleaning, and prompt maintenance services to ensure uninterrupted comfort and a healthy living environment.
Recreation & Common Areas
Well-equipped recreation rooms, TV lounge, indoor games, landscaped gardens, and common areas for relaxation, socializing, and community events.
Medical & Emergency Support
On-call medical assistance, first-aid kits, tie-ups with nearby hospitals, and emergency response protocols to ensure health and safety at all times.
Laundry & Housekeeping
Regular housekeeping, in-house laundry services, and well-maintained washrooms to provide a clean, hygienic, and hassle-free living experience.

Hostel Rules

To ensure a harmonious and safe living environment, all residents are expected to follow these simple rules. Your cooperation helps maintain discipline and comfort for everyone.

  • Curfew: 10PM weekdays, 11PM weekends.
  • Guests: 10AM–6PM.
  • No alcohol, smoking, drugs.
  • Quiet hours: 10PM–7AM.
  • Weekly room inspection.
  • Fees due by 10th every month.
